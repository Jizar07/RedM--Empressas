# Veterinary Recipes Collection

This file contains all veterinary recipes for animal care and feeding in the game.

## 🧬 Libidgels (Animal Breeding Aids)

All libidgels use the same base materials and produce 10 units each.

### Base Materials Required (All Libidgels):
- **Cápsula Plástica** (Plastic Capsule) x20
- **Rótulo** (Label) x20
- **Seringa de Vidro** (Glass Syringe) x4
- **Embalagem** (Packaging) x20

### Available Libidgels:

| Product | Output | Animal Type |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Libidgel Bovino** | 10 units | Cattle |
| **Libidgel Suíno** | 10 units | Pig |
| **Libidgel Aviário** | 10 units | Poultry |
| **Libidgel Caprino** | 10 units | Goat |
| **Libidgel Ovino** | 10 units | Sheep |
| **Libidgel Asineiro** | 10 units | Donkey |
